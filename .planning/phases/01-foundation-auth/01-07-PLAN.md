<decision_made_in_this_plan>
`01-CONTEXT.md` left one item to Claude's discretion: whether to add a client-side "confirm new password" field. **Decision: no confirm field.** The form has exactly the two fields the backend accepts. Rationale: the rider is authenticated and knows their current password (they just typed it), a mistyped new password is recoverable by changing it again, and adding a third field would put the form out of step with `ChangePasswordRequest` and with the "mirror the backend's validate tags exactly, don't invent stricter client-side rules" principle used everywhere else in this phase.
</decision_made_in_this_plan>
